## 2. THE PRICING MODEL (core of the whole job)

**We do not work from a set budget. We price around a $2 to $4 CPM.**

- CPM is calculated on the **median views of recent Reels**, never the average. One viral video pulls an average up and does not repeat.
- **Always exclude pinned Reels** when calculating the median. Pinned ones are all-time hits.
- Never price off "views in the last 30 days" that a creator quotes. That is their whole account across all posts. We buy one video.
- Formula: `fee = (median views / 1000) x CPM`

### How to pull a creator's median
Open `https://www.instagram.com/<handle>/reels/` in the logged-in Chrome, scroll to load about 20 reels, then read all view counts. Drop the first 2 or 3 if they show pin icons. Sort, take the median. Report median AND average, the gap is usually the whole story.

### Standard offer shapes
- **Flat:** median-based, aim $3 to $3.5 CPM.
- **Hybrid:** guaranteed base plus $2 per 1,000 views above a threshold, capped. Design the threshold so the effective CPM stays near $2.00 at every level.
- **Cross-post (same video to a 2nd platform):** pure performance, **$2 per 1,000 views, 30 days, cap $250** (Jacob's own rule). Facebook is always calculated **separately** and does not touch the Instagram cap.
- **Stories:** $2 per 1,000 Story views, measured **24 hours** after posting, cap $50.

### VIEW CLAUSE — for creators asking very high numbers (Jacob, 2026-08-29)
Do **not** counter a $1,500 ask with $150-200. The gap kills the conversation. Instead **accept their number and attach a view clause**.

`threshold total views = (their rate / CPM) x 1000`

At $3 CPM: $300 = 100K, $1,000 = 333K, $1,200 = 400K, $1,500 = 500K.
At $4 CPM: $1,500 = 375K.

## 3. CONTENT RULES (Jacob is strict about these)

1. **The video must NOT feel like an ad.** This is the whole point.
2. **App on screen within the first FIVE seconds**, but completely non-commercial. Best format: meal prep, scanning the meal, logging the protein. Nobody scrolls away from someone prepping food.
3. **No scripted call to action.** Do not ask creators to say "download the app".
4. **They do not need to say the name out loud or put it in the caption.** Curiosity works better. We can drop a comment asking "what app is that", they pin their answer at the top.
5. If sponsored posts underperform for them, the sponsored disclaimer can go at the **very bottom** of the caption.
6. **Do not show the app late.** By the second half, 60 to 70 percent of viewers are gone. Conversion matters more than views.
7. For exercise-video creators: **split screen** (workout on top, app in use at bottom, full screen on the app after 6 to 8 seconds), or a **1 to 2 second ab flex** with overlay text like "How I eat to get a six pack", then straight into meal prep.
8. Approved angles: "what I eat in a day", "how I track my protein to stay in shape", "my secret hack for hitting my protein", "a tool I found that actually helps", "this might actually help me lose weight".
9. No medical claims, no guaranteed weight loss claims.
10. Sponsored disclosure required (branded content toggle or visible mention).
11. **The standalone "@protein8official tag" requirement was DROPPED.** The tag now happens inside the Story reshare.

### The Story ask (lowered 2026-08-19)
Creator **reshares their own post to their Story** on the day it goes live, with a **link sticker** and a tag. Nothing new to film. Reels cannot carry a clickable link, so the Story is the only clickable path. Plus **Protein8 link in bio for the first 48 hours**.

## 5. PROCESS (never pay before the row is green)

7. Pre-post review: **factual and app-related corrections only**, never creative

### Usage rights (standard, do not deviate)
Included: organic reposting on Protein8's own channels, **6 months** from the posting date. Not included: paid advertising and whitelisting, always a separate agreement with separate compensation.
